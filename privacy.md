# Privacy Policy — FMP Tracker

*Last updated: October 16, 2026*


## Summary

FMP Tracker does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal
data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

## Data collection

The app collects nothing. There is no server, no account, no login, and no
analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. The developer cannot
see, access, or receive anything you enter.

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored locally on your device using standard iOS app
storage:

- Program selections (split, phase, day) and exercise swaps
- Sets, reps, weight, and completion status you log
- Session history: dates, duration, training location, and your notes
- Personal records derived from your logged lifts
- Symptom check-ins, if you choose to log them
- Body measurements (weight, body fat, tape measurements) and any body targets you set
- Food log entries, your macro targets, and your weekly meal plan
- Your training calendar, reminder times, and warm-up choices
- Your Find My Program answers
- Plans you build in My Plans, including any clinician name you enter
- App settings, including your home equipment list

None of this leaves your device unless you send it yourself (a CSV export, a
backup file, or a plan you share), or you use Apple's own iCloud device backup, which is governed by
Apple's privacy policy rather than this one.

## Apple Health (optional, off by default)

With Apple Health sync on, the app:

- **Writes** a summary of each completed workout (start time, end time,
  duration, and an estimated calorie count), and any body weight you type into
  the Body tab. Weights that came from Apple Health are never written back.
- **Reads** your height and most recent body weight, so you don't have to type
  them in.

| Health data | Read or write | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Body weight | Read and write | Fill in your profile; save weights you log |
| Height | Read | Waist-to-height ratio and nutrition targets |
| Workouts | Write; read with Watch data on | Record sessions; find your Apple Watch workout |
| Heart rate | Read, with Watch data on | Show heart rate for a session |
| Active energy | Read, with Watch data on | Show calories for a session |

The app requests nothing else from Apple Health.

With Apple Watch data on, the app also **reads** heart rate, active energy, and
workouts from Apple Health for the time window of each session you log, and
shows them with that session. This data is stored on your device with your
other session data and is never transmitted anywhere. While Apple Watch data is
on, the app does not write its own workout to Apple Health, because your watch
already records it.

Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, is never
shared with third parties, and is not stored in iCloud by this app. This
exchange happens directly between the app and Apple's HealthKit framework on
your device. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security
→ Health → FMP Tracker.

## Workout reminders (optional, off by default)

If you switch on reminders in the Calendar, the app asks iOS for permission to
show notifications, then schedules a reminder on each training day you pick.
These are local notifications created and delivered by iOS on your device. The
app does not use a push notification service, never requests a push token, and
sends nothing anywhere. Turn reminders off in the Calendar, or in Settings →
Notifications → FMP Tracker.

## Links you open

Some buttons open another app or website, such as a YouTube search for an
exercise demonstration, or Mail if you tap the contact address. The App sends
nothing itself; whatever you do in that app or site (for example, the search
term YouTube receives) is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

## Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used. No data
is shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party, because the app
does not collect data to begin with.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information
from anyone, including children.

## Your control over your data

- **Settings → Delete All My Data** removes everything the app has stored on
  your device, in one step. Deleting the app does the same.
- Individual entries can be deleted in the app at any time.
- Data already written to Apple Health is managed in the Health app.
- Like all app data, the app's data is included in your iPhone or iCloud
  backups if you use them.
- There is no account to delete, because none exists.
- You can export everything to CSV from Settings at any time. The export file
  is created only for sharing and removed from the app once you've shared it.
- You can save a complete backup to a file of your choice (Files, iCloud Drive,
  AirDrop) and restore it later. The app writes the file only when you ask,
  removes its temporary copy once you've saved it, and reads a file only when
  you pick one. The backup holds everything above, so keep it somewhere private.
- In My Plans you can send a plan as a file or import one someone sends you.
  Only the plan itself is in the file.
