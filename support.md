# Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback: **fmp-tracker-support@protonmail.com**

I aim to reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone only. FMP Tracker has no server and no account. Deleting the app
deletes its data, so export a CSV from Settings if you want a copy.

**Apple Health or Apple Watch data isn't showing.**
Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → FMP Tracker and confirm access is
turned on. Watch heart rate needs a strength workout running on the watch, or
the FMP Tracker watch app.

**A workout isn't in Apple Health.**
Turn on Settings → Apple Health in the app. With "Add Apple Watch data" on, the
app waits up to 15 minutes for a workout from your watch, then saves its own the
next time you open the app.

**The rest timer isn't on my Lock Screen.**
Check Settings → Lock Screen Rest Timer in the app. If it says it's turned off,
turn on Live Activities in the iPhone's Settings → FMP Tracker.

**The watch app isn't on my watch.**
Open the Watch app on your iPhone and install FMP Tracker under My Watch →
Available Apps.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. FMP Tracker provides general fitness information only. See the
[Terms of Use](../terms/).
